## Employee Hiring Insights Dashboard  
**Date:** August 2025  
**Project Type:** Independent Project  

### Objective  
Expand Microsoft’s Employee Hiring History report to deliver deeper insights into retention, workforce diversity, and hiring predictors using best practices in data modeling and business intelligence.

### Enhancements & Features  
- Added new report pages analyzing retention, diversity, and predictive hiring patterns  
- Built custom **DAX measures** for KPIs such as retention rates, hiring efficiency, and diversity metrics  
- Designed intuitive, interactive visuals for HR decision-making  
- Published the enhanced report to **Microsoft Fabric** for real-time access  
- Fully interactive browser-based experience

### Key Outcomes  
- Stronger insight into hiring bottlenecks and diversity gaps  
- Improved HR predictive analytics  
- A modern, shareable business dashboard aligned with enterprise BI standards
